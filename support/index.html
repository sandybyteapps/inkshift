Inkshift support

Questions, feedback or a print that isn't behaving? We're happy to help.

Contact Sandybyte

Email support@sandybyte.com. Please include your device, iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps; please leave out anything private.

How do I play?

Pick a stencil, drag or tap to position it on the 4×4 sheet, then press Print. Each print adds ink where the sheet is blank and erases ink where it is already inked. Match the target print to finish. Undo is always available, and hints can show which stencil or position to try next.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There is no account or cloud sync, so deleting the app removes your progress. Your device backups may include app data.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Rewarded videos are always your choice, for example to double the coins from a solve or get an exact hint. The free version also shows an occasional ad between levels. Studio Pass removes ads.

My Studio Pass isn't showing

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with, then open Settings in Inkshift and tap Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. Resetting your progress never removes a purchase.

Can I reset the game?

Yes. In Settings you can reset gameplay progress, or reset only the skill profile the game uses to pick puzzle difficulty. Your purchases stay intact either way.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
