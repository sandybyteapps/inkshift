Inkshift privacy policy

Inkshift: Print Puzzle for iPhone and iPad, by Sandybyte. Effective 17 November 2026.

Inkshift is a puzzle game. You do not create an account, and we (Sandybyte) do not run servers that receive your game data. Your progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, and Google may collect some data to deliver them, as explained below.

Data kept on your device

The game saves the following in its own storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Puzzle progress, your printbook, Ink Coins, daily bonus streak and settings.
  • A skill profile built from your recent puzzle attempts, used to choose how hard the next puzzle is.
  • A 30-day record of ad impressions and the revenue values the ad SDK reports for them. With Revenue-Aware Tuning switched on, this can make a small, bounded adjustment to puzzle selection. It never changes a puzzle you are playing or the daily puzzle, and never adds extra ads. Records older than 30 days are deleted automatically.
  • A cached record of your Studio Pass purchase, checked against Apple.

You can switch off Revenue-Aware Tuning and reset your skill profile, revenue record or progress at any time in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of this data. Resetting progress does not remove a purchase.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought Studio Pass, the game shows rewarded and between-level ads from Google AdMob. To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • other device and app information, such as device model, operating system, IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred) and app identifiers;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data about ad loading.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are requested. Separately, iOS asks whether Inkshift may track you. Saying no to either does not affect gameplay, puzzles or purchases; you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change the tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Google's use of this data is governed by its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Resetting data in the game does not delete data Google already holds.

Purchases

Studio Pass and any other in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which purchases you own so they can be unlocked and restored. See Apple's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run our own analytics or send your game data to our own servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Inkshift is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK and EU data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your game data, most requests are best handled by resetting or deleting the app, or by contacting Google about advertising data. You can also contact us using the address below, and you may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
